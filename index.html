Shaoxiang Wu (吴少湘)

Hello! I'm Shaoxiang Wu, a fifth-semester B.Sc. student in Information Systems at the Technical University of Munich (TUM).

Since April 2026, I have been a Research Assistant at TUM's Chair of Data Analytics and Statistics, led by Prof. Dr. Alexander M. Fraser. At the chair, I work under Dr. Lukas Edman on efficient language-model learning.

I'm interested in making each unit of useful intelligence less costly in data, computation, and model capacity.

Research projects

  1. BabyLM: Paired Rewrites for Small-Data Pretraining. Paired original sentences with meaning-preserving rewrites to improve learning from a limited text budget. Compared simplification, explanation, and paraphrasing across multiple text sources and language benchmarks.
  2. Induction-Circuit Initialization. Developed a weight-initialization rule for induction circuits in freshly initialized models by studying the weight-matrix structure of the corresponding circuits.

Publication
